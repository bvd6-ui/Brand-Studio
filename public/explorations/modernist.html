Behind the direction

Reference: Jan Tschichold, Die Frau ohne Namen (1927). Original artwork visually inspected on the museum website; artwork is not reproduced here.

The reference uses large compressed sans-serif lettering, asymmetric photo blocks, red geometry and directional lines. This study adapts its hierarchy and asymmetry rather than reproducing its artwork.

Design choices

An unequal two-column grid separates message from product; large sans-serif type establishes reading order; a limited palette and one geometric line keep the composition repeatable in CSS.

Intended audience effect

The clear sequence and unfilled space may support the wearer’s sense of authorship while letting everyday shoppers find the product quickly. This is a design hypothesis, not a measured result.

Risk

The restraint may feel impersonal or insufficiently literary.
