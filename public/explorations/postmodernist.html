Behind the direction

Reference: Wolfgang Weingart, Collage 9, from Nine Collages: The New Graphics by Weingart (1975). Original artwork visually inspected on the museum website; artwork is not reproduced here.

The reference combines heavy crosses, broken concentric arcs and fragmented images in a dense central montage. This study uses that experimental precedent for a postmodern approach to layering and competing visual languages.

Design choices

A serif headline quotes book typography against monospace labels; offset paper-like panels disrupt a single grid; broken arcs and a large cropped quotation mark suggest an unfinished reading. Controls and copy remain upright and unobscured.

Intended audience effect

The mix may invite curious book lovers to assemble their own meaning, echoing the wearer’s authorship. This is a design hypothesis, not a measured result.

Risk

Layering may distract from the product; the narrow version reduces overlap to protect reading order.
